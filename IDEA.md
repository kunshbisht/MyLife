# MyLife — Share your daily life with friends

## Description

**MyLife is a social media app built around real life, not screen time.**

It lets you effortlessly share what you're doing with the people you care about, without turning your life into content or encouraging you to spend hours scrolling.

MyLife is designed to make sharing **quick, authentic and meaningful**:

> **Live → Share → Remember**

You live your life normally.
You share small moments with your friends.
MyLife helps you remember those moments later.

Unlike traditional social media, MyLife doesn't try to maximize engagement. The goal isn't to keep you inside the app — **it's to get you back into your actual life.**

---

# Core Principles

### 1. Real life comes first

MyLife should never feel like something you need to constantly check.

* No infinite scrolling
* No autoplay
* No engagement-bait
* No pressure to post constantly
* No requirement to maintain a perfect profile
* Feeds have a natural endpoint

When you've seen everything relevant, MyLife should simply tell you:

> **You're all caught up. Go enjoy your day.**

### 2. Authenticity over perfection

MyLife isn't about carefully producing content.

Photos don't need to be perfect.
Posts don't need to be edited.
Life doesn't need to look impressive.

The app should feel like opening a window into your friends' actual lives.

### 3. Friends over followers

MyLife is about relationships, not popularity.

There should be no obsession with follower counts, likes or becoming an influencer.

Instead of building an audience, you build a **circle of people you actually know**.

### 4. Remember, don't just share

Everything you post eventually becomes part of your personal history.

Months and years later, MyLife should help you rediscover moments you would otherwise have forgotten.

# Features

## 📸 Instant Photo

The fastest way to share what you're doing right now.

Open the app → take a photo → share.

Optional information:

* Text
* Location
* Time
* Emoji
* Mood
* People you're with
* Circle/audience

The entire process should take only a few seconds.

---

# 📰 Life Feed

Instead of an algorithmically optimized content feed, MyLife presents a simple view of your friends' lives.

Example:

> **Today**
>
> **Alex · 12:41**
> 📸 At school
>
> **Sara · 13:18**
> ☕ Getting coffee
>
> **Daniel · 14:02**
> ⚽ Football
>
> **You · 14:37**
> 📸 Lunch

The feed should prioritize:

1. Friends
2. Recent moments
3. People you interact with regularly

No infinite feed.

Once you've seen everything:

> **You're all caught up.**

---

# 🕐 What Are You Doing?

A lightweight temporary status.

Examples:

* 🎒 School
* 📚 Studying
* ⚽ Football
* 🎮 Gaming
* 🎵 Listening to music
* 🍕 Eating
* 🚶 Walking
* 💤 Sleeping
* 🏠 At home

Statuses can automatically disappear after a period of time.

Users shouldn't have to write an entire post just to tell friends what they're doing.

---

# 🗓️ Daily Post

For moments that deserve more than a single photo.

Users can create a post summarizing their day.

Features:

* Multiple photos
* Caption
* Tags
* Hashtags
* People
* Location
* Time
* Emoji
* Mood
* Audience selection

A Daily Post could look like:

> **Saturday**
>
> ⚽ Football
> 🍕 Lunch with friends
> 🎮 Gaming
>
> **4 photos · 3 friends**

---

# 👥 Circles

Instead of followers, users organize their relationships into circles.

Examples:

* Close Friends
* Friends
* School
* Football
* Family

Every post can be shared with one or more circles.

This lets users control who sees different parts of their life without having to maintain a complicated privacy system.

---

# 🤝 Shared Moments

When multiple friends are together, they can create a temporary shared moment.

Example:

## ⚽ Football — September 12

**6 people · 23 photos · 18:32–20:04**

Everyone can contribute photos to the same memory.

Instead of six people separately posting the same event, MyLife creates one shared experience.

---

# ❤️ Meaningful Reactions

Reactions should emphasize **who cared**, not how popular something became.

Instead of:

> ❤️ 1,284

Show:

> ❤️ Alex, Sara and 8 others

Possible reactions:

* ❤️ Love
* 😂 Funny
* 😮 Wow
* 🙌 Nice
* 🫶 Support
* 😂

The exact number of reactions should not become the focus.

---

# 🔔 Respectful Notifications

Notifications should be useful rather than addictive.

Instead of constantly sending notifications:

> **While you were away**
>
> 4 friends posted
> 2 people replied to you
> 1 shared moment was created

Notifications can be intelligently bundled so users don't feel forced to check the app every few minutes.

---

# 📴 Take a Break

Users can tell their friends that they're offline.

> 🌙 **Kunsh is taking a break until tomorrow.**

While taking a break:

* No unnecessary notifications
* No pressure to respond
* Friends know you're intentionally away
* The app doesn't try to pull you back

Being offline should feel normal.

---

Users can browse:

* Weeks
* Months
* Years
* Places
* People
* Events

The longer someone uses MyLife, the more valuable their archive becomes.

---

# 🚫 The Anti-Algorithm

MyLife should deliberately reject the mechanics that make traditional social media addictive.

The algorithm should **not** ask:

> "What will keep this person online for another hour?"

Instead:

> "What would this person actually want to see?"

Prioritize:

* Real friends
* Recent moments
* Relevant shared experiences
* Important messages
* Memories

Avoid optimizing for:

* Maximum screen time
* Rage
* Controversy
* Infinite scrolling
* Viral content
* Artificial engagement

---

# 📵 No Infinite Scroll

The feed should have a clear beginning and end.

Once there are no more relevant posts:

> **You're all caught up.**

There is nothing else to scroll.

This creates an important difference between MyLife and traditional social media:

**The app doesn't need you to stay.**

---

# 👤 No Influencer Culture

MyLife should not encourage people to think of themselves as content creators.

The language should reflect this.

Instead of:

> "Create content"

Use:

> **Share your life.**

Instead of:

> "Build your audience"

Use:

> **Stay connected with your friends.**

Instead of:

> "Increase engagement"

Use:

> **Remember your moments.**

---

# 🔐 Privacy First

Because MyLife represents someone's real life, privacy should be fundamental.

Users should always understand:

* Who can see a post
* Who can see their location
* Who can tag them
* Who can interact with them
* What appears on their profile

Location sharing should be especially deliberate and never automatically public.

---

# 🧭 The MyLife Philosophy

MyLife isn't trying to become the place where people spend their lives.

It's trying to become the place where people **share and remember their lives**.

### Traditional social media

**Open → Scroll → Scroll → Scroll → Scroll → Close**

### MyLife

**Open → Share → See friends → Remember → Leave**

The ideal MyLife user doesn't spend three hours on the app.

They spend **two minutes sharing something meaningful**, then go back to whatever they were actually doing.

---

# The Vision

MyLife should become the social network that people use **without feeling like they're living inside a social network.**

A place where:

* Friends stay connected
* Small moments matter
* Nobody needs to be an influencer
* Nobody needs to chase likes
* Memories don't disappear
* Privacy is respected
* The feed eventually ends
* And real life always comes first
